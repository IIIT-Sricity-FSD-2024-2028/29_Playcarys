💡 Carys Points & Match Rules

By default PlayCarys awards points from the match outcome (win / draw / loss). Your game can set its own figure instead by passing carys_earned and xp_gained when it reports a result through the SDK: 

PlayCarys.submitResult({ outcome: 'win', score: 4242, carys_earned: 80 });

Use the SDK — DB.recordMatchResult() is internal to the platform and is not reachable from a game. Whatever your game reports is capped by the ceiling you set below, so pick a number that reflects a genuinely good run.

The most a single match of your game may award one player (0–1000). Reviewed before your game goes live.
